# Repository Finder: Background Information and Future Directions 

 Ecological and environmental research encompasses a wide variety of data types, including direct observations, monitoring data, environmental measurements, experiments, and long-term ecological datasets. These datasets may have specific requirements regarding metadata, accessibility, preservation, and reuse, making the selection of an appropriate repository an important part of data management.

The aim of the Repository Finder is to provide researchers with an overview of repositories relevant to ecological and environmental research, helping them make decisions about where to deposit their data. The selection of repositories is not limited to those specifically designed for ecological or environmental data. General-purpose repositories may also be suitable, especially when domain-specific alternatives are unavailable or do not meet the requirements of a specific dataset.

The development of the Repository Finder is guided by the ambition to promote FAIR data practices and support the long-term accessibility and reuse of data as part of the LTER-LIFE project. Repository suitability may depend on several factors, including the scientific domain, data type, metadata requirements, and access conditions. 

At its current stage, the Repository Finder provides an initial overview of potentially relevant repositories, compiled through a search of available repositories and input from WUR. This overview is intended as a starting point of potentially suitable repositories rather than a definitive assessment or a ranking of their suitability.

A future (potential) objective is to move towards providing more targeted recommendations based on clearly defined LTER-LIFE requirements. This would involve establishing a set of criteria reflecting LTER-LIFE's expectations regarding data management, FAIR principles, and repository quality. These criteria could consider aspects such as support for recognised metadata standards, long-term preservation, data accessibility, interoperability, and services relevant to ecological and environmental research.
